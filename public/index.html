The rule that runs the platform

No number without
a tier.

The resolver tries five levels of evidence in order and stops at the first hit. Whatever it finds, it tells you how it knows — measured, derived or estimated, with the uncertainty attached. Presenting an estimate as a measurement is the one thing this platform will never do.

Substance compliance

Every article,
against the list in force.

No onboarding project, no supplier campaign, no template. The BoM engine reads your bill of materials in whatever shape it left your ERP — then assesses every article for PFAS, REACH SVHC and RoHS. TSCA and Prop 65 are in scope; for EEA-market products they return not_applicable.

  • Per-article screening, tree-walked. Thresholds are applied at the smallest article level — the interpretation confirmed by EU case law, implemented rather than just cited.
  • Identifier validation first. In one real customer file, 27% of CAS numbers failed their own check digit. We validate before we screen — garbage in is caught, not propagated.
  • SVHC double-check. Your screening result is cross-referenced against suppliers' own legally mandated SCIP notifications. A conflict raises a flag no manual process would catch.
  • Dated list snapshots. Every screening records the regulation list in force at the time — so when a substance is added, you can answer both "what just became reportable?" and "was what we shipped clear under the list that applied then?"

Digital Product Passport

The registry is live.
Be years ahead of the mandate.

The EU DPP registry opened in July 2026 and the six harmonised EN standards are published. Passports for electronics become mandatory later — but the companies issuing standards-aligned passports now are the ones the market will trust when it does.

Live — 20 July 2026

EU DPP Registry opened — with a free public repository of data models.

Published — May–July 2026

Six harmonised EN standards (EN 18216, 18219–18223) — ECO360 passports are built against them from day one.

18 Feb 2027

Battery passport mandatory (EV, industrial and LMT batteries).

~2027–2030

Electronics follows via horizontal repairability and recyclability measures. The mandate arrives in stages — the readiness question arrives now, in every tender and every buyer questionnaire.
